## Decisions

Each was made during the work and is recorded here after the fact. All were driven by measurement;
the measurements are reproducible from the committed source.

**Decision 1 — crop `272,135,720,720`, excluding the wordmark.**
The shield spans x 385–875, y 163–830 in the source, so its centre is (630, 496). That box is centred
on it and is the largest square that clears the `PRism` wordmark and tagline, which begin around
y=865. The shield centre lands at 50%/50% of the crop. *Rationale:* text is unreadable at icon sizes
and costs the mark room.

**Decision 2 — icon background `#030305`, not the app canvas `#07070B`.**
Sampling the source gives `#030305` at the corners and `#020204` mid-field. Padding with `#07070B`
left a faint but hard-edged frame where pad met art, confirmed by reading pixels across the boundary.
*Note:* `adaptiveIcon.backgroundColor` remains `#07070B` and is never visible, because the Android
foreground is fully opaque (`hasAlpha: no`) and covers the background layer.

**Decision 3 — iOS icon is full-bleed (`IOS_SCALE=1.0`).**
An initial 88% inset truncated the white beam against the pad boundary and read as clipping rather
than composition. Android still insets to 62%, inside the ~66% the system guarantees visible, so the
circular mask cannot clip the shield.

**Decision 4 — splash background keyed to transparent.**
An opaque replacement would have swapped one rectangle for another: the splash image is centred on a
solid `backgroundColor`, and the artwork's background is not flat, so no single colour matches it.
Thresholds (luminance 6/20) come from the source: background ~3, darkest *visible* facets ~46, shield
and beams ~253.

**Known consequence, recorded rather than hidden:** some prism facets are themselves near-black and
key out too. They render as the near-black splash background, so this is invisible in practice — but
it is a real property of the asset, not a claim that nothing was lost.

### Android was verified at build-output level only

**Fact, and the most important limitation in this record.** Android was **not** launched. No Android
emulator or AVD is configured on this machine, and none was created. What was actually checked:

- `expo prebuild --platform android` completed, and
- the generated resources were inspected: `ic_launcher_foreground.webp` present at every density,
  `mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml` referencing `@color/iconBackground` and
  `@mipmap/ic_launcher_foreground`, `colors.xml` carrying `iconBackground = #07070B`, and
  `splashscreen_logo.png` over `splashscreen_background = #07070B`.

That is file-level and XML-level evidence. **It is not a rendering check.** Nobody has seen the icon
on an Android launcher or the splash on an Android cold start. The adaptive-icon safe-zone reasoning
behind `ANDROID_SCALE=0.62` is therefore an **assumption validated by arithmetic, not observation**.
